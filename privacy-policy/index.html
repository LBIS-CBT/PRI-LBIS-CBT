Privacy Policy

Lead British International School Computer-Based Testing

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Lead British International School Computer-Based Testing platform (“LBIS CBT”, “we”, “us” or “the platform”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when students, teachers and authorised school administrators use the platform.

1. Information we handle

Depending on how the platform is used, information may include:

  • Account and school information, such as names, email addresses, roles, class details and student admission identifiers.
  • Examination information, including examination settings, questions, answers, submitted responses, scores and related records.
  • Technical and activity information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the platform.
  • Google account information made available when a teacher chooses to connect a Google account, including the account's basic profile and email information.

2. Google account connection and Google user data

Teachers may choose to connect a Google account to use Google Forms-related examination features. When a teacher authorises the connection, LBIS CBT requests access to basic Google account identity information and read-only access to Google Forms content (the Forms body read-only permission).

We use this Google data only to identify the connected teacher account and support the platform's examination workflows, including retrieving Google Form structure and question content when the teacher requests an extraction. We do not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Google user data is not made available to other teachers or students for unrelated purposes. It may be processed by authorised platform administrators and service providers only as needed to operate, secure and support the requested platform functions.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use data obtained through Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Teachers should connect only an account they are authorised to use. A teacher can stop future access by disconnecting the Google account through the available account controls, where provided, and can also revoke LBIS CBT's access from their Google Account security settings. Disconnecting access does not necessarily delete examination records already created in the platform.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide role-appropriate access, create and administer examinations, retrieve authorised Google Forms content, process examination responses, display results, maintain records, provide support, and protect the platform against misuse or security incidents.

4. Storage, security and sharing

Platform records are stored using the services configured for LBIS CBT, including its Supabase-hosted backend. Google connection tokens are encrypted by the application before being stored. We use access controls and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Access to personal information is limited to authorised personnel and service providers who need it to operate or support the platform. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the school or the platform.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as it is reasonably needed for school administration, examination delivery, record keeping, security and the purposes described in this policy, or as otherwise required by applicable law or school policy. Retention periods may differ by record type. Requests to access, correct or delete information should be directed to the school contact below. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate educational, administrative or legal reasons.

6. Children and student information

LBIS CBT is used in a school setting and may process student information under the direction and authority of the school. Students should use the platform only as instructed by the school. Parents or guardians may contact the school with questions or requests concerning a student's information.

7. Your choices and requests

Users should keep their login credentials confidential and notify the school if they believe an account has been accessed improperly. Teachers may revoke Google account access through Google Account settings. For privacy questions or requests concerning platform information, contact the school using the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the platform or its data practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
